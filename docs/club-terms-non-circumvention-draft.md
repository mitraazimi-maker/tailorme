# DRAFT: tailor me Club terms, "Customers introduced through tailor me"

**Status: draft for legal review. Not in force and not shown in the app.**
Written in the plain-English voice of the rest of tailor me. A lawyer should check enforceability (restraint-of-trade principles in NSW and the unfair contract terms rules for small businesses under the Australian Consumer Law) before it goes into the Club terms.

---

### Customers introduced through tailor me

1. A customer is **introduced through tailor me** when they first contact you, or you first quote for them, through tailor me.
2. For **12 months** after the introduction, you agree to:
   - take further alteration and repair work from that customer through tailor me, using the **Rebook** button, and
   - not ask or encourage that customer to arrange work with you outside tailor me to avoid the booking fee.
3. You may still do work for that customer outside tailor me if **they** ask to, without encouragement from you. We don't expect you to turn anyone away.
4. Before a booking is confirmed, don't share your phone number, email, website or social handles in quotes, messages, your profile or photos. Your contact details are shared automatically once the customer books.
5. If a customer reports that you arranged, or asked them to arrange, work outside tailor me, we'll look into it. Depending on what we find we may:
   - dismiss the report,
   - give you a written warning, or
   - **suspend your Club membership for 6 months.** While suspended you won't appear in search, can't quote, your open quotes are withdrawn and your Club billing stops. Bookings you already have still stand. You can rejoin when the suspension ends.

   We'll always tell you the outcome and the reason. We won't tell you who reported it.

### Why this is fair to tailors

- You pay **no** booking fees or commission. The customer pays the booking fee, and a rebook costs them a flat $5.
- Bookings through tailor me add to your reviews and your "Booked through tailor me" count, which help you get found.

## Notes for the lawyer

- Point 3 is deliberate: banning all off-platform work with a customer is likely an unreasonable restraint. The obligation targets *steering* customers away, not serving them.
- Point 4 matches what the app already enforces (`src/lib/contact-filter.ts`), so the term and the product agree.
- Consider whether 12 months is reasonable for a $10–29/month membership.
- Point 5 reflects the owner's decision (1 Nov 2026) that tailors found booking introduced customers outside tailor me are suspended for 6 months. The app lets an admin warn instead, for weak evidence or a first offence. Check that the suspension (and keeping no refund for the current billing period) is proportionate, and whether the tailor needs a right to respond before a decision.
- The customer-side terms should say the booking fee pays for the introduction, and that rebooking through tailor me keeps their booking history, fit notes and the ability to review.
- Customer rewards terms are needed too: 10 points per booking, 20 per rebook, 50 points = a $5 voucher off a booking fee (never off the tailor's price), applied automatically, no cash value, and whether points expire (currently they don't). Say we can change the scheme with notice.
